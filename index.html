Spur gear

Generic gearboxes

Teeth run parallel to the shaft axis. Easy to make, efficient (~98%), but loud under load due to abrupt tooth engagement.

Helical gear

Car transmissions

Teeth cut at an angle. Multiple teeth engage at once, spreading load and absorbing noise. Generates axial thrust — needs thrust bearings.

Bevel gear

Car differentials

Cone-shaped gears that mesh at an angle (usually 90°). Used wherever a drive direction change is needed — car differentials split torque to left/right wheels.

Worm gear

Elevators · tuning pegs

A threaded screw (worm) drives a toothed wheel at 90°. Very high reduction ratio in a small space. Self-locking: the wheel cannot back-drive the worm.

Rack & pinion

Car steering systems · CNC machines

A circular pinion gear meshes with a flat toothed bar (rack). Rotating the pinion pushes or pulls the rack in a straight line — the core mechanism of car steering and machine tool tables.
